Teaser Audit Overview
City Group Hospitality

Your leadership talks about a culture built on the nobility of service, spread across eleven operations from fine dining to school cafeterias. None of that story lives on citygrouphospitality.com, where the homepage never names a single one of your restaurants.

Belief Gap Analysis
Where Belief Holds. Where It Breaks.
Trust Developing

The homepage asks visitors to "trust in the consistency" of City Group restaurants without pointing to a single restaurant, dish, or story that earns it.

Excitement Missing

Rouj Creole, City Pork, Spoke & Hub, Proverbial Wine Bistro, Beausoleil, City Slice: none of these concepts appear by name on the homepage a first time visitor lands on.

Confidence Strong

Eleven operations open and running, with new concepts like Proverbial Wine Bistro and Spoke & Hub launching within the last two years. The growth is real and visible on the ground.

Anticipation Developing

The News page exists, but the most recent post is at least three years old. Concepts that opened since then, including Spoke & Hub and Proverbial Wine Bistro, have no news presence on the site at all.

Emotional Investment Missing

COO Stephen Hightower and CEO Patrick Valluzzo have told the story of a company built on hospitality and belief in your people, but only in a local business magazine. That story never made it to your own site.

Priority Recommendation
You Already Have the Story. It's Not on Your Site.

The words your own leadership uses to describe City Group already exist, they just live in someone else's publication instead of yours. The fix isn't inventing a new story. It's moving the one you're already telling, in interviews and in person, onto the homepage your guests actually visit.
